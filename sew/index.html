Sew Patterner: free made-to-measure sewing patterns

Make sewing patterns to your measurements in your browser. Choose a garment and its style, a standard size or your own measurements, and print the pattern at home. Sew Patterner is free, open source, and needs no account or download.

Features

  • T-shirts, tank tops, shift dresses, fitted, circle and gathered skirts, pull-on trousers, bags, aprons, cushions, hats and a bodice block
  • Necklines, sleeves, lengths, shapes and fit for each design
  • Standard sizes or your own measurements, with several sizes nested on one pattern
  • Seam allowances, grainlines, notches, darts and labels on every piece
  • PDFs for home printers (A4 and Letter) and copy shops (A0), projector mode, SVG and DXF
  • Cutting layouts, how much fabric to buy, materials and step-by-step instructions

Guides and free patterns

  • Sew Patterner guides: getting started, taking your measurements, printing a PDF pattern and a glossary of sewing terms
  • Free sewing patterns: T-shirt, circle skirt, tote bag and more

For weaving, try Weave Patterner; for knitting, Knit Patterner. Sew Patterner needs JavaScript to run. Please turn it on to start designing.
