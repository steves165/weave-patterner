Knit Patterner: free online knitting chart maker

Design knitting charts in your browser. Paint stitches and colours square by square and read the written pattern as you go. Knit Patterner is free, open source, and needs no account or download.

Features

  • Standard chart symbols: knit, purl, yarn over, k2tog, ssk, centred double decrease, make 1, slip and twisted stitches
  • Cables: 1/1, 2/2 and 3/3 right and left crosses
  • Written instructions row by row, with repeats, for flat knitting or in the round
  • Stitch counts checked on every row, and long floats in stranded colourwork (Fair Isle) flagged
  • Gauge, finished size and how many stitches to cast on
  • A preview of the knitted fabric, colours from a picture, PNG export and printing

For weaving, try Weave Patterner, the weaving draft designer. Knit Patterner needs JavaScript to run. Please turn it on to start designing.
